About me

I am a PhD student at Fudan University, advised by Prof. Xipeng Qiu, and a member of FudanNLP. I received my bachelor’s degree from Hangzhou Dianzi University.

My research centers on foundation models and intelligent agents, with three main interests:

  • LLM Pre-training & Multimodal Integration: Learning general-purpose representations and integrating knowledge across language, vision, and audio.
  • Embodied Intelligence: World modeling, grounded reasoning, and learning to plan and act in physical environments.
  • Human–AI Interaction: Speech and audio understanding and generation for natural, real-time interaction.

My work has appeared at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, and CVPR. I also contribute to the open-source InternLM and MOSS model families.

I am a member of vidar-team and the dedicated photographer of @blueblueO908.
